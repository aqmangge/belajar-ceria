> Belajar Ceria kini sedang giat dibangunkan dan dioptimumkan untuk penggunaan di **Android TV / Smart TV Box**, tablet dan telefon pintar Android. Ciri navigasi remote D-pad, panduan tetikus (mouse), skrin pelancar (splash screen), dan rekaan bunyi ceria (Playful Sound UX) telah ditambah.

A colourful, playful learning app for children aged 3 to 7, designed for Android TV, TV boxes, tablets, and phones (Android 8 or newer).
No ads, no internet permission, no accounts: it works completely offline and the APK is tiny (~430 KB), because every picture, animation and playful sound is drawn or synthesised in code!

*Aplikasi pembelajaran untuk kanak-kanak: ABC, 123, Alif Ba Ta, kalimah Islam dan perkataan Arab, melalui kad imbasan, melukis, seret & lepas, padankan perkataan, serta Ujian Diagnostik Tahun 1.*

## 🎮 Kawalan: Remote TV vs Tetikus (Mouse)

Aplikasi ini direka khusus dengan sokongan dwikawalan pintar:

### 1. 🎮 Boleh Guna Remote TV Sahaja (D-Pad Arrows & OK)
Anak-anak boleh belajar santai di hadapan televisyen menggunakan alat kawalan jauh (TV Remote) biasa:
* **Menu & Subjek**: Navigasi pilihan kad menggunakan anak panah `⬅️ Kiri`, `➡️ Kanan`, `⬆️ Atas`, `⬇️ Bawah` dan tekan `🔘 OK`.
* **👀 Kad Belajar (Flashcards)**:
  * `⬅️ Kiri` / `➡️ Kanan`: Selak kad sebelumnya atau seterusnya.
  * `🔘 OK / Enter`: Ulang dengar suara sebutan (Voice TTS) dengan animasi kad melantun (jelly bounce)!
* **📝 Ujian Diagnostik Tahun 1 (Kuiz)**:
  * Anak panah memilih antara 4 pilihan jawapan.
  * `🔘 OK / Enter`: Menghantar jawapan, mendengar kesan bunyi sorakan gembira dan letupan confetti!
* **华语 Cakap Mandarin**: Mendengar sebutan, memilih dialog panda, dan latihan ucapan.
* **Tukar Tema**: Menukar antara tema 🌈 Rainbow, 🦸 Hero Squad, dan 👶 Kawan Comel.

### 2. 🖱️ Memerlukan Tetikus Tambahan (Mouse USB / Wireless)
Untuk aktiviti interaktif yang memerlukan koordinasi motor halus dan kursor sentuhan bebas:
* **✏️ Melukis & Menyurih (Trace/Draw)**: Kanak-kanak menahan klik tetikus untuk menyurih garisan berjalur huruf (A-Z), nombor (1-10), dan Hijaiyah (Alif Ba Ta).
* **🧩 Permainan Seret & Lepas (Drag & Drop)**: Klik dan seret jubin huruf/angka ke kotak slot bayang yang tepat.
* **🔗 Padankan Pasangan (Match Pairs)**: Klik dan tarik garisan penyambung dari gambar ke perkataan sepadan.
* **🎵 Muzik Bebas Interaktif**: Klik tali gitar, kekunci piano, dan palu dram/xylophone secara bebas.

> 💡 **Petua Ibu Bapa**: Sambungkan sebarang tetikus komputer USB standard atau tetikus wayarles (Bluetooth / USB Dongle) ke port USB di belakang Android TV atau TV Box anda. Kursor akan serta-merta muncul pada skrin TV!

## 🌟 Skrin Pelancar & Panduan Penggunaan

1. **Splash Screen Ceria**:
   * Menyambut anak-anak dengan animasi maskot melantun dan alunan muzik pembukaan (*tada fanfare*).
   * Butang pantas **🚀 Mula Belajar** (sedia dipilih dengan butang `OK` remote) dan **📖 Panduan Kawalan**.
2. **Panduan Kawalan (Control Guide)**:
   * Skrin panduan grafik yang menerangkan perbezaan aktiviti remote-only dan aktiviti yang memerlukan tetikus.
   * Lencana visual (**🎮 Remote OK** dan **🖱️ Perlu Mouse**) dipaparkan terus pada setiap jubin permainan dalam topik.
   * Peringatan mesra terapung (*floating animated toast*) akan muncul sekiranya aktiviti melukis atau seret dipilih: *"🖱️ Sambungkan Tetikus (Mouse) untuk aktiviti ini!"*.

## 🔊 Rekaan Bunyi Ceria (Playful Sound UX for Kids)

Semua kesan bunyi disintesis secara masa nyata tanpa sebarang fail audio luaran (menjimatkan saiz dan tiada lengah masa / zero latency):
* **Bloop & Bubble**: Bunyi buih comel apabila kursor remote menukar fokus butang.
* **Boing!**: Kesan lantunan kartun apabila maskot melompat atau kad digoncang.
* **Ta-da! & Fanfare**: Alunan muzik kejayaan apabila anak-anak menyelesaikan soalan atau memulakan aplikasi.
* **Pop & Tick**: Maklum balas taktil ceria pada setiap klik atau sentuhan butang.
* **Ding! & Chime**: Bunyi loceng ceria pada jawapan betul.
* **Oops!**: Maklum balas lembut tanpa mematahkan semangat apabila jawapan belum tepat.
* **Zap & Applause**: Bunyi laser superhero dan tepukan penonton dalam tema Hero Squad.

## What's inside

Five subjects, each with four ways to play:

| Subject | Items |
|---|---|
| **ABC** | A to Z, each with a picture: A is for Apple 🍎 |
| **123** | 1 to 10, with things to count, and the Malay word (Satu, Dua…) |
| **Alif Ba Ta** | The 29 Hijaiyah letters, named as in Malaysian Iqra' classes |
| **Islamic Words** | Bismillah, Alhamdulillah, Assalamualaikum, Masjid, Al-Quran… with their meaning |
| **Arabic Words** | كتاب Kitab (book), قطة Qittah (cat), شمس Syams (sun)… 20 everyday words |

| Activity | How it plays |
|---|---|
| 👀 **Learn** | Big flash cards, read aloud. Swipe them like photos on an iPhone; tap to hear again. |
| ✏️ **Draw** | Trace the dashed letter with a finger in a crayon colour. No need to colour it all in: following the letter's shape is enough (the app checks the crayon ran along most of the letter's centre line). Then it fills with colour and confetti flies. Messy scribbles get a hint to wipe with the sponge. |
| 🧩 **Drag** | Drag each tile into the slot of its picture: 🍎 takes A, three ducklings take 3, the shadow of ب takes ب. |
| 🔗 **Match** | Draw a line from each picture to its word (or tap one, then the other). |

**华语 Cakap Mandarin** (a card on the home screen) teaches Mandarin for speaking in front of people, in four steps:
24 words (greetings, name, age, family, school, likes, thanks), 16 short sentences, 4 role-play conversations with a
panda (meeting someone, at school, family, being asked to introduce yourself at an event: pick the right reply, then
say it), and 4 short speeches given on a stage with curtains, a spotlight and an audience (a teleprompter lights up as
the model reads each line, then the child says it; the audience stands and claps at the end). Simplified characters
with Hanyu Pinyin, meanings in Malay and English, read by the phone's Chinese voice. The microphone uses the phone's
own speech recogniser to give up to three stars; without one it records the child and plays it back. Nothing heard
is kept or sent.

**🎵 Muzik** (top left of the home screen) is a break from learning: a piano (C4–C6, chords and slides), a drum kit
(kick, snare, hi-hats, three toms, crash), a guitar (six strings in standard tuning, chord buttons C G D Am Em F, tap a
string or swipe to strum) and a rainbow xylophone (C5–C7, slide for a glissando), with ♪ ♫ floating up from each note.
Every sound is synthesised from how the real instrument vibrates (additive piano with inharmonic strings and two-stage
decay, Karplus-Strong guitar with fractional-delay tuning, modal xylophone bars, modelled drums), in equal temperament
at A4 = 440 Hz: measured off the device, the guitar is within 0.2 cents of pitch, the xylophone 0.8, the piano 2.

**📝 Ujian** (the last card on the home screen) is the Year 1 diagnostic test (Ujian Diagnostik Tahun 1) as a
game: Bahasa Melayu, Bahasa Inggeris, Matematik and Jawi, 25 marks each, in the school paper's five question
types per part (vowels, picture to word, syllables, capital letters, "Ini ___"; capital to small letters, beginning
sounds, missing vowels, colours, "This is a ___"; counting, number sequences, adding, taking away, shapes; Jawi letter
names, look-alike letters, alphabet order, picture to Jawi word, Jawi spelling). The child answers one question at a
time with big buttons; at the end each part gets a mark out of 25 with the paper's band (21–25 Cemerlang, 15–20 Baik,
10–14 Perlu bimbingan, 0–9 Perlu intervensi segera), a total, and a list of wrong answers for the parent. A button at the top switches the
instructions, shown and read aloud, between 🔊 Melayu, 🔊 English and 🔇 Senyap (remembered). Questions are
drawn fresh from banks each time, and the last mark of each part is kept.

Three looks, picked with the round buttons at the top of the home screen and remembered: **🌈 Rainbow**
(the default), **🦸 Hero Squad** (bold comic colours, a city skyline, "POW!" celebrations, and a squad of four
original kid heroes drawn in code: Kapten Ceria, Puteri Bintang, Wira Khaizuran and Si Kilat, who wave from every
game's top bar, run and fly across the screen, zap each right answer with a star-laser and fly by together at the end
of a round; never during the test, so it gives nothing away) and
**👶 Kawan Comel** (soft nursery pastels, rising bubbles, heart confetti, and four babies from the owner's own
artwork, cut out in `res/drawable-nodpi`, who sit in every game's top bar and now and then swing on a swing, munch a
donut, play with a ball or sneeze; one pops up and sends hearts at each right answer, and all four bounce up when a
round is won).
They change every subject and every game. The heroes are original designs and the babies are the owner's own artwork: no characters or logos from films,
comics or cartoons, which belong to their studios.

The feel is meant to be like Apple's: spring physics on every movement (buttons press in and bounce back,
cards follow the finger and tilt, tiles lift with a deeper shadow), soft drifting colour blobs behind
everything, glossy cards with tinted shadows, zoom transitions between screens, gentle bell sounds and haptics.

Words are spoken with the phone's own text-to-speech. Arabic uses an Arabic voice if the phone has one
(Settings → System → Languages → Text-to-speech → install Arabic); otherwise the transliteration is read
in English.

## Get the APK

Every push that touches `kids-learn/` builds the APK on GitHub Actions (workflow **Belajar Ceria (kids app)**).
Open the latest run and download **belajar-ceria-apk**; the **belajar-ceria-screenshots** artifact shows every
screen running on an emulator.

On the phone or tablet: open the APK, allow "install unknown apps" for the browser or file manager when asked,
and install.

## Build it yourself

Needs JDK 17 and the Android SDK (platform 34, build-tools 34.0.0):

```sh
./gradlew assembleRelease
# app/build/outputs/apk/release/app-release.apk
```

`tools/screenshots.sh <apk> <folder>` installs it on a connected device or emulator, opens every kind of
screen and saves screenshots. `tools/playtest.py <folder>` then plays the games like a child (flicks cards,
drags tiles, draws match lines, traces Alif), records a video and checks each game reacted; CI runs both. Any screen can be opened directly:
`adb shell am start -n com.belajarceria.kids/.MainActivity --es open 2,3` (subject 0–4, activity 0–3).
